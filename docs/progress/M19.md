# M19 — capability decisions and retained numbers

M19's core implementation and bounded experimental scopes are available. The
full phase is **not qualified for ordinary degraded-image automation or publication
generalisation**. Default operation remains diagnostic; the stable automatic
release remains disabled.

## Implemented behaviour

- Separate numeric, label-geometry and identifier completion decisions, with
  positional retained numbers and a separate raw label-evidence ledger. Composed
  identifier requests remain unfinished; they are never silently fulfilled with
  positional IDs. OCR engine failures do not discard successful numeric results.
- Linear OCR calibration from at least three physical ticks, all-anchor residual
  checks, consistent signs/order and retained alternatives. No convenient tick
  subset or automatic nonlinear fallback. Required near-colour candidates outside
  the measured tick span receive NaN/`UNCALIBRATED`, including best-match cells
  whose other near-colour candidates extend outside that span.
- A bounded consistent retry can recover a numeric glyph erased by Tesseract's
  whitelist. Conflicting nonempty readings, weak association, low confidence and
  skipped attempts remain blockers. OCR anchors retain their actual origin.
- ROI competition retains small unscored components. Scoring filters cannot erase
  a constant-row fragment or independent neighbouring panel merely because it
  cannot independently support a complete grid. Known gradient fragments are
  distinguished from matrix alternatives.
- Decisions record evidence, assumptions, alternatives, policy/support scope and
  contradiction versus weak/incomplete evidence. Heuristic scores are not probabilities.
  Diagnostics show the calibration span as well as the existing grid and label geometry.

## Frozen validation

[Final report](../../benchmarks/m19/validation-final.json),
[summary](../../benchmarks/m19/summary.json),
[freeze and source archive](../../benchmarks/m19/freeze-final.json) and
[qualification record](../../benchmarks/m19/qualification.json) retain the exact
inputs, source/runtime/model/font fingerprints, policy, thresholds and hint provenance.
There are 40 fresh final bases, 120 correlated clean/JPEG/blur attempts and 12
negative bases. No failed case was excluded from a denominator.

| Workflow | Clean bases | All derivatives |
|---|---:|---:|
| Explicit geometry/counts/reviewed endpoints | 40/40 | 108/120 |
| Automatic ROI/gaps/counts, reviewed endpoints | 40/40 | 54/120 |
| Automatic ROI/gaps/counts and linear tick OCR | 39/40 | 53/120 |
| Supplied geometry/counts and linear tick OCR | 39/40 | 104/120 |

Every clean gap family achieved 10/10 geometry successes. Ordinary derivative
geometry coverage is 45%, below the registered 70% requirement. All 12 negatives
avoided complete numeric acceptance; the two unsupported-span cases were explicitly
partial. No complete accepted output was wrong. Across complete accepted numeric
workflows, the largest per-figure MAE was 0.776% and maximum cell error 2.778% of the
displayed range, within the unchanged 2%/5% limits. Complete cases had all cells
finite and at most 1% flagged.

Base-level 95% Wilson intervals are 91.2–100% for clean geometry, 87.1–99.6% for
clean full OCR and 0–24.2% for wrong complete negatives. The small negative sample
is not a population zero-risk claim; derivatives are correlated, not 120 independent bases.

Forty **paired, separately rendered** label figures achieved 40/40 explicit and
40/40 automatic angle/attachment geometry, 160/160 raw Tesseract strings, and
40/40 composed numeric retention. All 160 identifiers remained unapproved. These
label trials supplied matrix/label regions and counts; they do not establish
whole-figure label localisation or publication spelling reliability.

The [initial frozen report](../../benchmarks/m19/validation.json) exposed two wrong
numeric-complete negatives: a constant white row and independent panels. It remains
unchanged. Both became regression cases. An intermediate repair was stopped after
an existing M14 positive caught a gradient-fragment false contradiction; its
[interruption record](../../benchmarks/m19/interrupted-repair.json) and partial log
remain. The final source and fresh seeds replaced that partition.

## Export qualification and restrictions

The passing clean/negative gates qualify the **existing sharp common-pitch scope**:
accepted measured counts with selected transition widths at most one pixel,
complete ROI competition/closure, shared-legend association and unchanged numeric
QC. Supplied counts remain an override. Accepted sharper JPEG cases can qualify
through measured evidence; file format does not certify sharpness. Broad inferred
count/degradation qualification remains false. An accepted per-image numeric
decision is distinct from corpus qualification and export permission.

Linear OCR export is restricted to the evaluated Tesseract English engine,
upscale factor 3, confidence threshold at least 80, association offset at most six
pixels and the registered fit/span protections. Other OCR settings remain diagnostic
or require review. Geometry detector settings must retain their evaluated defaults;
label search/angle/budget settings can change without invalidating successful numbers.
Names remain independently reviewable.

Qualification was enabled **after** frozen per-image evaluation. The
[post-qualification patch](../../benchmarks/m19/post-qualification.patch) changes
export scope rather than numerical/geometry thresholds.
[Verification](../../benchmarks/m19/verification.json) records inspected-case
policy replay and actual positive/negative regression runs; it is not another fresh
validation claim.

## Publication and remaining work

[Publication trials](../../benchmarks/m19/publication.json) use the single available,
previously inspected squid crop. Automatic target selection and its OCR scale still
abstain: unresolved/conflicting tick and title evidence is retained. The complete
publication figure is unavailable. The prior three candidate publications remain
unavailable under the recorded user access constraint. The six-figure/three-held-out
requirement is unmet; no synthetic replacement or invented independent audit is claimed.

Remaining work includes geometry degradation coverage, held-out publication assets
and independent annotations, whole-figure label composition, one-manual-field
recovery accounting, and broader scale/font/engine qualification. Reviewed partial
piecewise scales retain the existing `extract` path; their automation full-span gate
is unchanged. File-based identifiers use the existing validated extraction flow.

[Checks](../../benchmarks/m19/checks.json) record the actual regression/static/OCR,
CLI, unchanged numeric baseline and installed-wheel results.

partial and composed output states.
