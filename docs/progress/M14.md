# M14 — Palette-diverse reconstruction and numeric-QC audit

Implemented after M13 using [spec_5](../specs/spec_5.md).
M15 has not started. Numeric thresholds and experimental export scope remain
unchanged. Palette names/LUT truth are never inputs to the recogniser.

## Changes

The core still compares cell colours to the **observed bar** in CIELAB and maps
bar positions through the configured scale. New per-cell evidence retains every
connected near-match component, inclusive sample indices, normalised positions,
and minimum/maximum actual calibrated values. quality.csv adds a JSON
colour_candidate_evidence column; provenance and automation decisions summarise
local, broad-contiguous and disconnected near-matches. These are engineering
tolerance sets, not statistical confidence intervals. Broad/disconnected spans
still flag and produce NaNs under the previous total-span rule.

Three structural fixes improve useful acceptance without increasing colour/QC
thresholds:

- Broad dark data bands are retained. Perpendicular stroke exclusion requires
  thin original-pixel width and neighbouring contrast. Border trimming requires
  a thin strip followed by nonuniform interiors; complete dark sample columns
  are not eroded.
- Supplied-count bar association samples median cell interiors, avoiding
  black gridline contamination. Unresolved counts retain the previous bounded
  image-bin sampling; no new count acceptance policy was introduced.
- Smooth changing low-contrast bar gaps can join across a dark/neutral centre.
  A contained fragment is suppressed only when a closed full core continues
  through its internal endpoints with measured smooth changing pixels.
  Suppression evidence is retained. Separate competing bars remain blockers.

Runtime versions: detector palette-structure.1, core-profile.2,
review-acceptance.2, component diagnostics observed-bar-components.1.
Numeric tolerance/fraction thresholds, numeric-only export scope, supplied-count/
supplied-or-reviewed-scale requirements and identifier review are unchanged.

## Historical failure audit

[Per-image/per-cell audit](../../benchmarks/m14/corrections-audit.json) covers
all **31** original mode-1 numeric failures: **23** excessive-flag decisions and
**8** missing/ambiguous-only decisions. Their overlapping reasons are not
independent failure counts.

There are **537 ambiguous cells**, **zero poor-distance flags** and **zero
missing-pixel flags**. Near-match sets include **352 broad contiguous ranges**
and **185 disconnected sets**. All selected ROI bounds equal the annotations,
and manual bounds produce the same ambiguity counts; these failures are not
caused by bad ROIs or masked black/white cells.

Clean/blur cell interiors match plotted RGB exactly in these failures. JPEG
can change sampled cells by up to three RGB units and median bar samples by
one unit; the report separately retains cell and bar mismatch measurements.
Even clean rounded gradients can have broad low-gradient near-match ranges.
Disconnected sets are not automatically proof of non-injective original palettes:
quantisation and noise can split near-matches, while axial endpoint blur can
create an extra branch by blending the bar endpoint with its background.

The worst retained nearest candidate in a flagged cell is **21.69%** wrong
relative to the displayed range (validation-06-blur, row 7, column 1), despite
best ΔE76 = 0 and unchanged cell RGB. Its disconnected bar-position span is
22.77%. Thus removing ambiguity flags or accepting every small-distance
candidate would admit materially wrong values. No such relaxation was made.

[Inspected corrections regression](../../benchmarks/m14/corrections-regression.json):
supplied-count/scale complete acceptance improves **33/72 → 34/72**, with one
new correct complete decision, zero lost complete decisions, zero wrong complete
decisions, and zero exports/acceptances on eight applicable negatives. This is
inspected development regression, not fresh validation.

## Conditional manual numerical accuracy

**0.2077%**). The six standard/explicit unambiguous schemes produce **24/24**
fully finite unflagged clean matrices. Their maximum cell error is below 0.77%.
The custom self-approaching gradient remains partly unresolved and is reported

## Examples, checks and limits

[Worked commands](../../examples/m14/README.md) cover supplied-count/scale
viridis automation and manual teal–black–red reconstruction with verified fixed
legend direction/range. Both produce 288/288 finite cells and zero QC flags.
The synthetic teal–black–red anchors are (0,180,180), (0,0,0), (210,0,0)
at 0, 0.5, 1 with linear RGB interpolation; no publication-wide palette is named.

Full OCR-enabled suite: **524 passed, 18 subtests passed**. Ruff and strict mypy
passed with NumPy 1.26.4 and 2.5.4. Source-distribution/wheel builds, isolated
wheel smoke checks and both worked CLI examples passed. A post-validation
Optional-value narrowing repair in QC serialization passed 89 focused tests
and preserved both worked QC files byte-for-byte; it changed no policy or
recognition result. The original validation source freeze and exact typing-only
patch are retained separately from the final source freeze.

Remaining failures include uncertain grid extents, broad raster boundary peaks,
low-contrast magma endpoints on dark backgrounds, touching borders and genuine
numeric ambiguity. The 70% usefulness target is still unmet. Dark/saturated
centres, endpoint clipping and transformed scales require the review described
in [palette documentation](../palettes.md). Colour compatibility cannot recover
raw RNA-seq measurements, verify identifiers or authorise a broader export mode.
